# Development Priority Intelligence

Track 1: AI for Digital Public Infrastructure & Governance (BRICS Theme: Innovation)

## Overview
A scalable, multilingual AI platform designed as a Digital Public Good (DPG) that aggregates citizen development requests across diverse linguistic regions, fuses them with national infrastructure, demographic, and financial data, and surfaces explainable priority recommendations alongside a before-and-after project impact measurement engine.

## Problem Pillars
1. **Fragmentation**: Multi-channel, multilingual citizen demand disconnected from government GIS and administrative data.
2. **Misprioritization**: Lack of explainable, multi-factor scoring linking demand to real infrastructure deficits.
3. **No Impact Measurement**: Inability to assess whether funded/completed projects actually reduced citizen demand.
