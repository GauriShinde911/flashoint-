# Pitch Deck Outline — Development Priority Intelligence (Track 1)

10–12 Slides designed for the BRICS Innovation Theme and National Governance Track.

---

### Slide 1: Title Slide
- **Title:** Development Priority Intelligence (DPI)
- **Subtitle:** Fusing Multilingual Citizen Feedback with National Public Infrastructure Data & Impact Auditing
- **Theme:** Track 1: AI for Digital Public Infrastructure & Governance (BRICS Theme: Innovation)
- **Tag:** Designed as an Open Digital Public Good (DPG)

### Slide 2: The Three-Fold Problem
- **Problem 1: Fragmentation:** Millions of citizen voices trapped in regional languages across disparate silos.
- **Problem 2: Misprioritization:** Infrastructure investments disconnected from localized real-world deficits.
- **Problem 3: The Post-Funding Blindspot:** Zero automated mechanisms to measure if completed digital or physical infrastructure actually solved the citizen problem.

### Slide 3: The Solution — Development Priority Intelligence
- Unified ingestion pipeline (Voice, SMS, Messaging, Web).
- Data fusion combining citizen demand, Census 2011 + NFHS-5, National Hospital Directory, and PMGSY/JJM outlays.
- Deterministic scoring paired with grounded Gemini justifications.
- Closed-loop before/after impact verification for public expenditures.

### Slide 4: AI & Agentic Architecture (Gemini + ADK)
- Multilingual intent extraction, translation, and semantic clustering without third-party APIs.
- Strict architectural guardrail: **Code computes the math; Gemini explains the justification.**
- Eliminates AI hallucinations in financial and public policy recommendations.

### Slide 5: Data Integrity & Real Government Sources
- Live integration with `data.gov.in` (National Hospital Directory, Health Centres Directory, PMGSY).
- Honest demographics methodology: Explicit disclosure of Census 2011 baseline + NFHS-5 projections.
- Full data transparency: Badges indicating `REAL DATA` vs `SYNTHETIC DEMO DATA`.

### Slide 6: Novel Analytical Engines
- **Priority Scoring Engine:** Multi-factor index balancing demand, population vulnerability, infra deficit, and existing budgets.
- **Silent Need Detector:** Identifies digitally excluded communities with acute deficits but low complaint volume.
- **Investment-Demand Mismatch:** Highlights fiscal leakage and overlooked hotspots.

### Slide 7: The Game Changer — Impact Measurement Engine
- Direct response to the brief's mandate to "measure the impact of large-scale initiatives".
- Temporal pre/post-commissioning analysis of citizen demand curves.
- Quantifiable ROI for taxpayers and policymakers.

### Slide 8: Multi-Region Command Center Demo
- Interactive geospatial visualization across states (Maharashtra & Uttar Pradesh pilot districts).
- Natural language query interface for national decision-makers.

### Slide 9: BRICS Cross-Border Adaptability
- Generic `Admin 0` to `Admin 3` modular geographic schema.
- Reusable across Brazil, South Africa, Russia, and India with zero backend refactoring.
- Fosters South-South cooperation through shared public goods.

### Slide 10: Digital Public Good (DPG) Compliance
- 100% open source (MIT License).
- Standardized open schemas (JSON Schema / CSV).
- Designed for zero-cost deployment on free-tier infrastructure (FastAPI on Render, Frontend on Firebase Hosting/Static, free Gemini API tier).

### Slide 11: Scalability & Future Roadmap
- Phase 5 expansion: Vertex AI AutoML demand forecasting & citizen photo-evidence verification via Gemini multimodal.
- Integration with national grievance portals (CPGRAMS, state CM portals).

### Slide 12: Team & Conclusion
- Driving accountability, transparency, and data-driven equity in public governance.
- Call to Action: Deploy DPI across BRICS digital public infrastructure networks.
