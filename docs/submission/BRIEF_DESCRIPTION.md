# Brief Description (2–3 Lines)

### Option 1 (Standard / Official Submission):
Development Priority Intelligence (DPI) is an open-source, multilingual AI platform designed as a Digital Public Good that aggregates citizen infrastructure requests via voice, text, and messaging in regional languages. Powered by Google Gemini 1.5 Flash and deterministic analytics, DPI fuses citizen demand with national demographic baselines (Census 2011 + NFHS-5) and official facility registries (data.gov.in) to prioritize public investments, detect underreported silent need, and measure the real-world impact of completed public works across BRICS nations.

---

### Option 2 (Punchy / High-Impact):
DPI bridges the critical gap between grassroots citizen demand and national infrastructure spending. Using Google Gemini for multilingual voice understanding and deterministic scoring engines fused with real government datasets, it surfaces priority hotspots for policymakers and introduces the first automated impact measurement engine to evaluate public expenditure post-completion.
